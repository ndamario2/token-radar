Farm plans

Every board row carries six steps: a fresh wallet, two protocol-specific actions sized to the protocol's TVL, one qualifying action a week, a hold of 30–60 days, and a claim rule (claim only from the protocol's own published link, never pay to claim). Each step states its cost and its time. A plan is concrete because the action, the capital and the chain change with the protocol.

What this is not

This board does not predict whether or when a token is issued. A protocol "records no token" in the snapshot; that is not proof it has none. Implied value is this entry's own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is.
